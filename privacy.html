Privacy Policy

What stays on your device, what you choose to submit, and which services are involved.

Updated: 8 November 2026

Who operates this website?

SIRAI is an independent Sudan-focused initiative in development, not a registered NGO. This website provides educational planning tools and project information. SIRAI does not currently solicit donations.

Information stored on your device

WaterShare, ProjectStarter and TaqaWise run calculations in your browser. The tools do not automatically submit your plans or calculations to SIRAI. Your browser may store drafts, language preferences and the low-data setting locally. The service worker also stores website files for offline use. You can clear this information through your browser settings. Downloaded files remain on your device unless you share them.

Information you choose to submit

If you use a feedback or community idea form, its contents and any optional email address are submitted to Formspree, a third-party form provider. Formspree processes submissions according to its policies. Do not include sensitive personal details, exact water-point locations, movement schedules or identifying information about other people. We cannot guarantee individual replies or immediate removal from third-party systems.

External services and links

SIRAI is hosted with GitHub Pages. When low-data mode is off, some illustrative photographs may load from Unsplash, exposing ordinary request information to the image provider. Low-data mode substitutes local illustrations. When you follow links to external resources such as ReliefWeb, IOM, WHO or social media, those sites have separate privacy practices. Hosting providers may independently maintain technical access logs.

Analytics and advertising

SIRAI does not currently include third-party advertising pixels or automatic visitor analytics in its website code. This statement does not control technical logging performed by hosting, browser or third-party service providers.

Cookies and offline storage

SIRAI does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. The site uses browser storage for your selected language, optional drafts and low-data preference, and Cache Storage to support offline pages. These features do not transmit your drafts to SIRAI. External providers, including GitHub Pages, Google Fonts, Wikimedia and Unsplash, may process ordinary request information under their own policies. We have not enabled nonessential marketing trackers, so there is currently no marketing-cookie consent banner. This must be reviewed if tracking or third-party functionality changes.

Children's information

The tools do not require an account or age details. SIRAI's voluntary feedback and idea submission forms are intended for adults aged 18 or older. Do not provide personal information about children or other people. If you believe such information was submitted, request its review or removal using the contact method below.

Access, correction and deletion requests

To ask about, correct or request deletion of a submission you sent, contact SIRAI directly at the email below. Include only the approximate submission date and the email address you used, if any. We may need to verify that the request concerns your own data. We will review and act on requests for information under our control, and where necessary contact Formspree. Removal from third-party backups may be subject to provider rules and applicable retention requirements. Do not send identity documents or sensitive information in your first message.

Email a privacy request · Formspree privacy policy

Your choices and questions

You can use the planning tools without creating an account. You can change language and low-data preferences, clear local browser data, and decide whether to submit feedback. For privacy questions or requests concerning a feedback submission, use the feedback page and include enough information to identify your own submission, without sharing unnecessary sensitive details. We will review requests but cannot promise removal from systems outside our control.
