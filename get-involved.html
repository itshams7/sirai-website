Get involved in testing

Help make SIRAI’s free tools clearer and safer. Right now participation means voluntary feedback, not a job, appointment, or formal volunteer role.

Pre-launch information. SIRAI is not a registered NGO and does not accept donations.

01

Test a planning tool

Load a fictional example, change a value, and describe what works or fails.

02

Review a translation

Flag awkward Arabic, French, German or English phrasing. Include the page and the exact words.

03

Suggest an improvement

Explain a practical problem, who the tool could help, and which assumption needs review.

Participate safely

Do not submit full names of other people, exact water locations, travel schedules or sensitive circumstances. Feedback is optional and handled through Formspree. No reply is guaranteed.
