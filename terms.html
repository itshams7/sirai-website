Terms of Use

Please understand the limits of these free tools before relying on calculations or published information.

Last reviewed: 5 November 2026

What SIRAI currently provides

SIRAI is an independent initiative in development, not a registered NGO or emergency-response organization. The website offers preliminary educational resources, practical worksheets and reference links. It does not provide guaranteed assistance, grants, field services or professional consultation.

Use of planning tools

WaterShare estimates, project budgets and household energy calculations depend on the assumptions and values entered. They are not a substitute for verified local supply data, engineering design, safeguarding review, financial advice or qualified on-site assessment. Check calculations independently before using them for decisions that may affect safety or essential services.

Sudan Explorer and outside information

State boundaries on Sudan Explorer are illustrative. Links to external reports help you begin research, but do not establish that a water point, clinic, aid organization or any other service is available or safe in a given location. Verify the source, date, area covered and current details with the original publisher. Do not use the Explorer as an emergency response service.

Safety and responsible sharing

Do not share sensitive water locations, movement schedules, identifiable volunteer details or private information about other people through SIRAI tools or feedback. If you download or share a plan, review and remove sensitive details first. Use tools lawfully and with appropriate community consultation, consent and professional review where required.

Availability and updates

SIRAI's tools may change, contain mistakes or be temporarily unavailable, including when connectivity is poor. Offline features require successful loading while connected and may differ by device. Check our development updates and known-issues register for limitations. No field outcomes or partnerships should be inferred unless explicitly documented.

Feedback and further information

For corrections, concerns or technical issues, use the feedback form. SIRAI may review and improve the website but cannot guarantee a reply. Read the privacy policy before submitting information.
