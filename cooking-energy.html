VERIFIED NATIONAL CONTEXT

Having access doesn’t mean being able to use it.

National figures show how many people had access to electricity and clean cooking in Sudan in 2024. They do not tell us whether a household can rely on electricity during cooking hours or afford cooking fuel today.

Before considering electric cooking

    Never overload wiring, bypass protection or use indoor charcoal and fuel-burning devices without appropriate ventilation. Have wiring and inverter/battery installations assessed by a qualified professional.

    HOW TO INTERPRET THE RESULTS

    What is verified, modelled or still unknown?

    Verified external data

    The two national access percentages above are World Bank 2024 observations. WHO identifies electricity and LPG as clean cooking options at the point of use.

    Modelled estimate

    Electric consumption = rated power (kW) × session hours × sessions per day; monthly energy assumes 30 days. It is a scenario, not a measured meter reading.

    Missing or user-supplied data

    Current local Sudan tariffs, cylinder and charcoal prices, outage schedules, device efficiency, backup capacity and fuel stock are not automatically verified or updated.
