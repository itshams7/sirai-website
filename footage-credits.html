Sources behind SIRAI's media.

SIRAI's homepage uses previously published archival video. The scenes show real people but were not filmed for SIRAI and do not demonstrate projects delivered by this initiative. The dates and locations below distinguish historical footage from present-day conditions.

Children playing outdoors in Sudan

Date: 15 March 2019. Creator: Evworo. License: CC BY-SA 3.0.

Original file on Wikimedia Commons. This recording predates the current conflict and is used as a scene of ordinary childhood, not footage of the present humanitarian crisis.

Sudanese refugees arriving in Chad

Date: 16 May 2023. Creator: Henry Wilkins / Voice of America. Rights: Public domain in the United States, as identified on the source page.

Original file on Wikimedia Commons. The recording documents Sudanese people displaced across the border into Chad.

Jawaher's story after fleeing Sudan

Date: 20 June 2013. Creator: Doctors Without Borders / Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF-USA). License: CC BY 3.0.

Original file on Wikimedia Commons. This story concerns a Sudanese mother who fled to South Sudan in 2013. It is historical footage, not material from the current conflict.

Fonts and graphics

The interface uses Inter and Instrument Serif through Google Fonts, plus local SVG interface graphics. The fonts are loaded from Google's service when a compatible connection is available. For details, refer to each font's current licence on Google Fonts.

For the homepage presentation, videos are muted, shown in an approximately 0.92× playback-rate sequence, cropped to fill the hero, and crossfaded. SIRAI is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original creators, journalists or aid organizations. Video is omitted when reduced-motion or low-data preferences apply.
