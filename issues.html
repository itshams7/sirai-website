Known issues & limitations

What is not yet verified, which functions require a connection, and what we need to test next. The list is not an automatic live bug database.

Open limitation

Independent field validation

Planning assumptions and real-world usefulness have not yet been validated with community users. Code-level calculation checks are not field evidence.

Known limitation

Remote photographs require internet

The local resource pages and scripts are cached, but images served from external providers may not appear without a connection.

Known limitation

Feedback submission requires internet

The feedback form uses Formspree and cannot deliver submissions while disconnected.

Testing needed

Mobile browser and accessibility review

iPhone Safari checks have informed fixes. Independent testing across low-cost Android devices and assistive technologies remains outstanding.

Found a reproducible bug?

Report the page, browser, steps taken, expected result, and actual result. Avoid names, locations, movement times and private project details.
