CLEAR ABOUT OUR WORK

Transparency, safety and your privacy.

SIRAI is an independent Sudan-focused initiative in pre-launch testing. This page explains what is available today and where the limits are.

Our current status

SIRAI is in development. The online planning tools are educational drafts, not verified field interventions. SIRAI is not a registered NGO and is not collecting donations.

Founder experience

Community activities described in the founder biography refer to work carried out before SIRAI was established. They are background, not evidence of SIRAI delivering those projects.

What happens to your information

WaterShare, ProjectStarter and TaqaWise run in your browser. Tool drafts and calculations are not sent to SIRAI by these tools. Local browser storage and downloaded files remain on your device; sharing or sending a file is your choice.

Feedback and third parties

The feedback form sends the details you enter to Formspree for SIRAI to review. Email is optional. Formspree may process and retain submissions under its own privacy policy. Please avoid names of third parties, exact sensitive locations, schedules or other identifying details.

Read Formspree’s privacy policy

Tool limitations

Water estimates depend on accurate household counts and verified supply. Project plans need local consent and safeguarding. Energy and solar estimates are preliminary, not electrical designs or installation guidance. Obtain local professional review where safety requires it.

How to improve SIRAI

If a calculation looks wrong, a translation is confusing or something does not work offline, please report it. We review feedback to improve the public tools, but cannot guarantee individual replies.

Analytics and public issue reporting

SIRAI currently does not run automatic third-party visitor analytics. Optional feedback is sent through Formspree when you submit it. For known limitations, see the public issue register.
