Practical planning guide

Three simple ways to use the free SIRAI tools. Educational worksheets only: no field validation, professional endorsement, or promised services.

Keep identifying details and sensitive collection schedules private.

Water coordination

Enter the number of households, proposed daily litres per household, and collection windows. Compare the resulting demand with locally confirmed supply and tank capacity. Avoid including names or specific locations in public reports.

Do not treat estimated litres as a medical or humanitarian minimum. Verify safe water quality, access, and local agreement.

Community project budgeting

Describe the problem and who should be consulted before adding costs, responsibilities, timeline, and risks. Replace fictional prices with locally verified quotations.

A completed draft is not an approved project and does not guarantee funding or consent.

Household energy inventory

Use actual appliance labels for watts, estimate hours of use, and identify essential loads. Adjust battery and solar assumptions to understand sensitivity.

Battery capacity, wiring, protection, thermal conditions and electrical safety need qualified review.
