SIRAI WaterShare · Resource 01

Community water-planning toolkit

A simple planning worksheet for shared collection schedules and daily water-volume estimates. Built for discussion and adaptation, not as a substitute for local technical or public-health guidance.

Planning inputs

Number of households included in the proposed arrangement.
Planning assumption only. Adjust based on local needs and qualified guidance.
Optional comparison; tank volume does not prove daily water availability.

Planning estimates

2,430 LTotal planned daily volume
1,215 LVolume per collection window
27Households per window (even split)
0.49×Planned volume / tank capacity
Important limitations

This calculates volumes only. It does not assess drinking-water safety, source yield, pump capacity, accessibility, fairness, queue time, or whether storage refills. Community members should agree on appropriate allocations and schedules; consult local public-health and technical expertise before operational use.

Planning checklist

  • Agree on the purpose and participants before setting a schedule.
  • Check actual supply, refill timing and physical access with qualified local people.
  • Decide on collection times together and provide a way to raise concerns.
  • Assign maintenance responsibilities and revise arrangements when conditions change.
