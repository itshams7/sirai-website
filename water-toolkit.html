03 / REVIEW TOGETHER

A good plan considers people first.

Use the checklist before agreeing on any real collection arrangement.

01

Listen to everyone

Discuss access with households, including people with mobility limitations and those far from the collection point.

02

Verify the supply

Check source yield, tank refill times, pump output and whether the proposed volumes can actually be delivered.

03

Protect water safety

This tool cannot assess contamination or make water drinkable. Follow qualified local public-health guidance.

04

Keep improving

Agree on responsibilities, create a way to raise concerns and revisit the plan as circumstances change.

Important limitations

WaterShare does not verify water quality, safe per-person water needs, pumping capacity, queue times, source reliability or equity among households of different sizes. It is a discussion and calculation aid, not operational or health advice.
